About PromptNest

PromptNest was built with a simple goal: help people get better results from AI tools by helping them write better prompts.

Most people's first attempts at using tools like Midjourney, DALL-E, ChatGPT, or Claude are short and vague — a couple of words, or a one-line request. That's usually enough to get a response, but rarely enough to get a genuinely good one. AI models respond to detail: composition and lighting cues for image generators, and clear roles and structure for chat assistants.

PromptNest takes a simple idea — a subject, a style, a level of detail — and expands it into a properly structured prompt using techniques that actual prompt writers use by hand. The goal isn't to replace your creativity, but to give you a stronger starting point.

What makes PromptNest different

The tool covers two of the most common prompt types in one place: image generation prompts and chat/writing prompts. It's free, requires no account, and runs directly in your browser.

Who this is for

Whether you're new to AI tools and not sure where to start, or an experienced user who wants a faster first draft to refine, PromptNest is built to save you time.
