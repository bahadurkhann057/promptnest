Why a good prompt matters

The difference between a flat AI result and a striking one usually comes down to the prompt. Vague input gives vague output — specific, well-structured prompts give AI models the context they need to produce something genuinely useful.

Image prompts

Weak:

"a cat"

PromptNest:

"A close-up studio portrait of a Siamese cat, dramatic side lighting, shallow depth of field, hyper-detailed fur texture, cinematic mood"

Chat prompts

Weak:

"write a business plan"

PromptNest:

"Act as a startup consultant. Write a one-page business plan for a mobile coffee cart, including target customers, startup costs, and a 6-month revenue goal."
