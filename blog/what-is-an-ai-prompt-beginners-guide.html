What Is a Prompt? A Beginner's Guide to AI Prompts

Published August 2026

If you're new to AI tools, you've probably seen the word "prompt" everywhere and wondered what it actually means. This guide breaks it down in plain terms.

The simple definition

A prompt is just the instruction or question you give an AI tool. When you type something into ChatGPT, or describe an image you want Midjourney to create, that text is your prompt. The AI reads it and generates a response based on what you asked for.

Why prompts matter so much

AI models don't know what you're picturing in your head — they only know what you tell them. A prompt like "a dog" could produce almost anything: any breed, any setting, any style. A prompt like "a golden retriever puppy playing in autumn leaves, soft afternoon light, photorealistic" gives the model a much clearer target, and the result reflects that clarity.

Two broad categories of prompts

Most AI prompts fall into one of two families:

Image prompts — used with tools like Midjourney, DALL-E, and Stable Diffusion. These describe a visual scene: subject, style, lighting, composition, and mood.

Chat prompts — used with tools like ChatGPT and Claude. These describe a task: what you want written, explained, summarized, or solved, often along with a tone or format.

What makes a prompt "good"

A good prompt is specific rather than vague, gives context the AI needs to understand your goal, and states the format or style you expect back. It doesn't need to be long — it needs to be clear.

A simple example

Vague: "write about dogs."

Specific: "write a 200-word beginner's guide to choosing a dog breed for a first-time owner living in an apartment."

The second version tells the AI exactly who the reader is, what the goal is, and roughly how long the response should be — so there's far less guesswork involved.

Getting started

The best way to get comfortable with prompts is simply to write a few and see what comes back, then adjust. Small changes — adding a style, a tone, or a length — often make a bigger difference than starting over from scratch.
